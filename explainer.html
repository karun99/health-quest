Health Quest

An ethical, transparent food-education game that helps school students (Classes 8–12) learn to identify the healthiest food — with a visible Health Score and real nearby eatery ratings, built on zero-cost, privacy-first technology.

“Health Quest is a static, offline-first web app. No backend, no database, no tracking. Users bring their own AI keys. The whole thing costs ₹0 to run — forever.”

1 · The problem

School students face a flood of diet misinformation on social media. Food content is full of fear, fads and hidden ads. Meanwhile, food apps rank restaurants with black-box scores nobody can verify. Kids need to learn how to judge food themselves — not just obey a score.

📱

Misinformation

Viral diet myths travel faster than facts. Nobody teaches kids how to verify nutrition claims.

🔢

Black-box ratings

Big food apps never reveal why a place scored well — you must simply trust it.

😰

Fear-based learning

Most “diet” apps use timers, streaks and guilt. For vulnerable students, that pressure backfires.

3 · The transparent Health Score

Inspired by the Michelin Guide, which earns trust by publishing its criteria. Every food gets a score out of 100 built from 5 honest criteria — and a “Why this rating” button on everything.

4 · Locate Nearby — real data, honest rating

The app fetches real eateries from OpenStreetMap within 2 km of the user's PIN code (or a safe one-time location lookup). Each venue gets a rating from 100 on 4 transparent criteria — and every rating can be opened to see its own breakdown.

🍽️ Eatery type
20 pts
🚶 Near-enough
30 pts
🥬 Healthful options
20 pts
🗓️ Honest info
30 pts
⚠️ Judges, please verify: enter any real Indian PIN code and the app will show living, breathing eateries with hours and phone numbers from OpenStreetMap — not sample data.

5 · Safety by design (the ethics layer)

Health Quest was designed after studying three case studies, and every lesson maps to a concrete design choice.

Case studyWhat we learnedHow Health Quest applies it
Pokémon GoLocation features can cause real-world harm.Consent-first flow, no silent tracking, PIN-code alternative.
Blue Whale challengeManipulative mechanics harm vulnerable kids.No timers, no streaks, no “you must go”.
Michelin GuideTrust comes from transparent criteria.Visible score breakdown + “Why this rating” button.

6 · Technology (all free)

🌐 Frontend

Single HTML file · vanilla JavaScript · no framework · works offline from a plain file.

🗺️ Data

Local food database + OpenStreetMap Overpass API via a serverless proxy.

🤖 AI hints (optional)

OpenRouter → NVIDIA NIM → offline fallback. Bring Your Own Key, stored in localStorage.

☁️ Hosting

Netlify or Vercel free tier with a 20-line serverless function.

7 · Privacy promise

🔒

No API keys in the HTML (BYOK only). No backend database. No user tracking, no analytics, no cookies. Location used once and never stored. Keys live in localStorage and vanish when browser data is cleared.
