🛡️ Why this promise exists

Health Quest was designed after studying three famous case studies.

Pokémon Go (2016)

Location games caused real-world harm and trespassing. Our fix: consent-first flow, a pincode alternative, and a single one-time lookup.

Blue Whale challenge

Manipulative mechanics targeted vulnerable teens. Our fix: no timers, no streaks, no "you must go". A wrong answer is a lesson, not a loss.

Michelin Guide

Its trust comes from published criteria. Our fix: a visible Health Score with a "Why this rating" button on everything.

🏆 How venue ratings work (transparent)

Every eatery score is built from 4 visible criteria — just like the food scores.

🍽️ Eatery type
20 pts
🚶 Near-enough
30 pts
🥬 Healthful options
20 pts
🗓️ Honest info
30 pts

Restaurants earn more for type; fast-food earns less. Places advertising vegetarian, vegan or organic dishes earn healthful-option points. Places with opening hours, a phone number and a website earn honesty points. Distance is judged in kilometres. ≤ 2 km search radius.
